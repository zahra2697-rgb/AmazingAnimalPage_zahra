Lions





About my animal

Lions are large, powerful carnivorous cats that live in family groups called prides.
They live in grasslands, savannas, and open woodlands. Most wild lions live in parts of Africa south of the Sahara, with a small protected population of Asiatic lions living in India's Gir National Park and Wildlife Sanctuary.
Lions are the most social of all wild cat species. They live in groups called prides, which usually contain 10 to 15 members, including related females, their cubs, and a small number of adult males. (National Geograghic)


Fun Facts

  • Female lionesses do 85% to 90% of the hunting for the pride, using teamwork to catch fast prey.
  • A male lion's roar reaches up to 114 decibels and travels as far as 5 miles (8 kilometers).
  • Lions rest and sleep for up to 20 hours a day to conserve energy for the night.

How lions hunt

  1. Lions usually hunt in groups called prides, which increases their success rate
  2. Lionesses do most of the hunting because they are lighter and faster than males.
  3. Some lions circle the flanks to separate target animals from the herd, while others wait in the center for the attack.
