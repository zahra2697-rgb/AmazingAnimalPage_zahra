Put your animal's image(s) in this "assets" folder.

Rules for file names:
- all lowercase
- no spaces (use a dash - instead)
- example: lion.jpg, blue-whale.png, emperor-penguin.jpg

In your HTML, link to it like this:
<img src="assets/lion.jpg" width="300px" height="200px" alt="A lion resting in the grass">
